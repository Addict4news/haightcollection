About this project

Saving Stories Before They're Lost

The Haight Collection is a personal historical research project dedicated to exploring the people, places, and records of the past.

From nineteenth-century maps and old homesteads to family histories and newspaper accounts, this archive will bring together discoveries and source materials in one place.

More records and interactive map features are planned.
